# Point 0.4.0

Point 0.4.0 makes Settings easier to navigate and lets you preview appearance changes before your next capture.

- Find shortcuts, screenshot appearance, and annotation defaults in separate sidebar panes. Point remembers the last pane you opened.
- See changes to background padding, border color, and annotation style in the preview beside the controls.
- Hover over the selection overlay options to see the capture wash. Hover over Sunset, Lagoon, or Twilight to try the color, then click to save it.
- Open Point’s GitHub repository or website from the bottom of the sidebar.

Requires macOS 14 or later. The universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Unzip `Point-0.4.0-macOS.zip` and move **Point.app** to Applications. Use
`SHA256SUMS` to verify the download.

# Point 0.2.0

Point 0.2.0 adds curved arrows, screenshot previews, and backgrounds that stay set between captures.

- Drag a selected arrow's diamond to move its curve along or away from the arrow. Move it near the straight line to snap the arrow straight. Double-click an arrow to add or edit its caption.
- After copying a capture, use the thumbnail at the bottom left of the screen to save the PNG or drag it into an image or file input. After saving, choose **Show in Finder** to reveal the file. The preview closes after six seconds, with the timer paused while you hover, save, or drag.
- Choose your desktop wallpaper, another locally available macOS wallpaper, an Aurora, Ocean, or Sunset gradient, or a custom image as the screenshot background. Point remembers the background and its visibility across captures and launches.
- Press `G` on the annotation canvas to toggle the backdrop.
- The optional three-finger capture gesture recovers after sleep, session changes, or an interrupted trackpad listener.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
